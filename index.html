02 · Our story

A thousand stories. One celebration.

India is a tapestry of languages, traditions, flavours, and art forms. Afterglow invites you to wander through that richness, discover something new and connect through shared experiences.

From lively performances and handcrafted creations to the irresistible aroma of street food, the spirit is simple: come curious, leave inspired.
